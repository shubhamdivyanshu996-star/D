A Little Reminder That You're Loved 💗

01. Dear Dora 🌷

Hey Doraaaaa, I don't really know where to begin, but I know why I'm writing this. I heard about what happened, and I wish I could make you understand something that you might not be able to see right now. One argument, one difficult day, or one moment of feeling like you've failed cannot define an entire person. And especially not you. So consider these pages a little reminder from someone who is genuinely thankful that you exist in his life. Your Proton has something to say. ♡

🌷 🧸 🌷

02. You're Not A Failure

Dora, Please don't let a disagreement convince you that you're failing at life. You're human. You have emotions, expectations, limitations and days when things don't go as planned. Sometimes we say things we don't mean. Sometimes we misunderstand people we love. Sometimes we don't know how to handle a situation. That doesn't make us bad people. You can acknowledge your mistakes without turning them into your identity. You can apologise when necessary without believing that you're worthless. Please learn to separate what happened from who you are. Because you are so much more than one argument.

🦋

03. If Only You Could See Yourself

I genuinely wish you could borrow my eyes for just a minute. Maybe then you'd understand why I appreciate you so much. You keep questioning yourself, but I see someone whose existence has made a difference in my life. Someone who made ordinary conversations memorable. Someone whose presence became a little part of my everyday happiness. You might notice your flaws first, but I don't look at you as a collection of mistakes. I see a human being who deserves patience, kindness, respect and love. And I hope one day you learn to see yourself with that same kindness.

💗 🌸

04. You Don't Have To Be Strong Always

Listen, Dora. You don't have to be the strong one every single day. You don't have to pretend everything is fine when it isn't. You don't have to keep smiling just because people are used to seeing you smile. You're allowed to be tired. You're allowed to cry, take a break, ask for help and admit that something hurts. Being strong doesn't mean never struggling. And please remember, you don't have to earn affection by being useful, successful or perfect. You deserve kindness even when you have absolutely nothing to give.

☁️ 🌷 ☁️

05. About Your Little World

I know that sometimes relationships at home can become complicated. Especially when the people you love are involved. But please don't allow a difficult relationship or a disagreement to become the measurement of your entire worth. You and your sister are two different individuals, both learning and growing. There will be disagreements. There will be misunderstandings. You can love someone and still disagree with them. Give yourself time to understand what happened. Give her time too. You don't have to solve every complicated feeling today. One day at a time, Dora. ♡

🕊️

06. What You Mean To Me

Now comes something I don't think I say enough. Thank you for entering my life. You might consider yourself just another person going through ordinary days, but you became someone genuinely important to me. You changed me in ways I probably cannot explain properly. Remember how I say you changed me from an electron to a proton? Well, there's a reason behind that silly little comparison. You brought a different kind of positivity into my world. And I'm genuinely grateful for every conversation, every laugh, every random moment and every little memory. I'm glad I got to know you, Dora. More than you probably realise.

⚛️ ❤️

07. On Your Difficult Days

Whenever you feel like you're not enough, please remember: You are allowed to make mistakes and still deserve respect. You are allowed to have a bad day without calling yourself a failure. You are allowed to grow at your own pace. You are allowed to change your mind. You are allowed to ask someone to listen without feeling guilty about it. And you are allowed to receive the same care that you give others. I cannot promise that every day will be beautiful. But I hope you never forget that a difficult day doesn't make your whole life a difficult one. There's still so much waiting for you.

🌻

08. A Promise From Your Proton

I don't know what the future holds for either of us. Life might take us through different roads. Circumstances might change. Our routines might change. We might have disagreements and misunderstandings too. But I don't want you to ever feel that your value in my eyes depends on how perfectly everything goes. I'll always wish good things for you. I'll always want to see you growing, succeeding and discovering how much you're capable of. And whenever you need a reminder of your worth, I hope you remember that you have a friend who genuinely believes in you. No conditions of perfection. Just genuine friendship and care.

🤍

09. Read This Twice, Dora

If someday you forget how special you are, read this again. If someday you feel like you've disappointed everyone, read this again. If someday you think you're difficult to love, read this again. You don't need to become someone else to deserve happiness. You don't need to achieve something extraordinary to justify your existence. You don't need to be everyone's favourite person. Just be yourself. Keep learning. Keep growing. Keep giving yourself chances. And please, if things ever feel too heavy, talk to someone you trust. You deserve support beyond these pages too. The world is much bigger than the problems you're facing today. And your story is nowhere near finished.

🌸 🦋 🌸

10. Happyyy Diwali, Dora! 🪔

Happyyy Diwali, Doraaaaa! 🪔✨ I hope this Diwali brings you the kind of happiness that stays long after the lights fade. May your home be filled with warmth, your heart with peace, and your days with countless reasons to smile. I hope every little diya reminds you that even the smallest light can make a difference in the darkest surroundings. May Maa Lakshmi bless you with happiness, prosperity and good health, and may every new beginning bring you closer to the life you dream of. Forget your worries for a little while. Enjoy the sweets, the lights, the decorations and all those little moments. You deserve beautiful things, Dora. And this Diwali, my wish isn't just that you shine brightly for everyone else. I wish you find peace and happiness within yourself too. Lots of love and warm Diwali wishes from your Proton! ⚛️

🪔 🌸 ✨ 🪔

11. Till I'm Alive ❤️

Dora, If there's one thing I want you to take away from these eleven pages, it's this: Whatever life takes away, whatever circumstances change, and wherever our roads lead... Till I'm alive, you're always loved. I GUARANTEE THAT. You'll always have someone who sees you with love, believes in you, and is genuinely grateful for your existence. You are not a failure. You are not a burden. You are not someone whose worth can be decided by a bad day. You're my Dora. And I hope you never give up on yourself. Thank you for being a part of my life. With all the appreciation, care and friendship, YOUR PROTON ⚛️ Forever grateful for you. ♡
